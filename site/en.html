Get back
what was yours.

If an app was acquired with your Apple Account, it may still be available for download after disappearing from the Russian App Store.

  1. Connect your iPhone over USB and tap Trust.
  2. Sign in to the purchaser's account only on the computer.
  3. Select the app and install it.

Apple verifies the license and decides whether to serve the package. We do not promise to restore every app or download unknown copies.

Your data.
Yours alone.

Perenos has no server of its own, no ads and no hidden analytics. Apps and purchases are downloaded directly from Apple, and your files stay on your computer.

How security works

  • Protected session

    The session key is protected by Windows DPAPI or macOS Keychain. Your password is not stored.

  • Open implementation

    Both the app and ipatool source code are available for inspection.

  • Verifiable builds

    SHA-256 and Sigstore for releases. They do not replace Apple or Windows publisher signatures.

  • Local library

    IPAs stay with you. They may contain the purchaser's email, so do not publish them.

Before you start

Need more help? Ask on GitHub.

Can I restore an app removed from the App Store?+

Sometimes, if your Apple Account owns it and Apple still serves it to that account. Find it in purchases or Russian services, select it and install.

How do I restore VK Music?+

Connect your iPhone, sign in to the purchaser's account on your computer, find VK Music in purchases or Russian services and install it. Apple verifies the license when downloading.

Does Perenos work on a Mac?+

The new Avalonia version shares its codebase between Windows and macOS. Apple Silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 15 or later are supported. Test DMGs are marked -test and are not notarized; Gatekeeper may warn about an unidentified developer.

Can I install an app without an Apple Account?+

Downloading requires the purchaser's account on the computer. A saved IPA installs without signing in again on the computer, but iOS may request the purchaser's account at launch or update. FairPlay is not bypassed.

Are messages and app settings transferred?+

No, only apps are transferred. Restore their data using iCloud, a backup or the app's own synchronization.
